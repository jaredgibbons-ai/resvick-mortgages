Full button-by-button audit of https://whiteridge.ca (Valley Financial Specialists / DLC broker Jarrett White, BCFSA licence #504751), walked live in-browser Oct 30, 2026 (10:02–10:47 PM PDT). Every page reachable from the main navigation, footer, and in-page links was visited and every button/CTA mapped to its exact destination and behavior; every multi-step flow (5 readiness quizzes, the guided assessment router, the Lender Match quiz, both Calendly bookings, the Broki mortgage application, the Broki mortgage tracker, both calculator pages, and the referral form) was walked step by step.

No form was submitted, no booking was scheduled, no account was created, and no payment was made: flows were stopped before their final submit/send/schedule/sign steps, and those final steps are documented instead. Fake placeholder data only (e.g., "Test Test", test@example.com) was used where a flow needed input to advance.

**Why this is in Josh's package:** White Ridge's quiz system is the working model behind the readiness-quiz spec in CALCULATORS.md — a 2-question router feeding five acronym-branded scored quizzes, results shown BEFORE the lead form, capture via an "Unlock full report" form. Two of its five quiz front doors are broken (see E1) — renewal and investor, two of Josh's priority clusters.
