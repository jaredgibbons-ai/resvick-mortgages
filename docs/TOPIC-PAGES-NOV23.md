# Topic pages added Nov 23, 2026

Six pages: construction and self-build, second and vacation homes, retirees and seniors, student loans, the stress test, and the glossary. All pass the readability gate (grade 5.4 to 6.4) and validation. Facts that come from a source are listed here; items marked CONFIRM are lender practice that Josh should check before launch.

## Sources used
- OSFI, Minimum qualifying rate for uninsured mortgages (greater of contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%; reviewed at least annually).
- CMHC quick reference and Second Home page (qualifying rate, 39% GDS and 44% TDS, year-round occupancy, 2 insured properties per borrower, $1.5M limit, 25-year amortization with 30 available through Home Start, progress advances for new construction).
- BC Housing and BCFSA, Homeowner Protection Act information (Owner Builder Authorization, exam, 10-year liability, selling rules, 2-5-10 warranty periods).
- Canada Guaranty progress draw page (owner-builders eligible, no single sub-trade contract at 50% or more of cost, title in borrower's name, lender manages holdbacks).
- Government of Canada news release, March 2023 (interest on Canada Student Loans ended April 1, 2023); StudentAid BC (provincial interest ended February 19, 2019).
- NerdWallet Canada and RBC construction pages (draw stages, interest-only during construction, inspections paid by the borrower, typical 20% or more down).

## CONFIRM with Josh
- Construction: which of his lenders do construction and self-build, typical down payment he sees (page says "20 percent or more", "some ask for more on a self-build"), whether the loan converts automatically or needs a new application.
- Second homes: which lenders handle water-access, seasonal and resort property; whether he sees insured second-home files; the page says a seasonal cottage "usually" needs conventional financing.
- Retirees: how his lenders count RRIF, annuity, investment income and any asset-based programs; the page says "often counted" and "lenders vary".
- Student loans: how his lenders count a loan not yet in repayment (page says "many lenders still count a calculated payment"); whether lenders accept a repayment assistance plan payment.
- Stress test: whether any of his credit union lenders skip the test (page says "lenders vary").
- Glossary: definitions are generic; he may want to change wording on prepayment, IRD and collateral charge to match his lenders.
